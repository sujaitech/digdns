What the report covers

Website. Whether it’s up, how fast it answers, its redirects and security headers, and what it’s built with.

Registration and hosting. Registrar, expiry and locks, who hosts the site, and who handles its email.

DNS and email authentication. Every common record, nameserver health, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC with builders to fix them.

Mail servers, blocklists and SSL. MX reverse DNS, MTA-STS, whether your servers’ IPs are on spam blocklists, plus certificate expiry and allowed issuers.

When to use this

After you change a DNS record, resolvers keep serving their cached copy until its TTL runs out. This shows which ones already have the new answer, and how long the rest may hold the old one.
