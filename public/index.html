What the report covers

Registration and hosting. Registrar, expiry and locks, which server serves the website, and which service handles its email.

DNS records. A, AAAA, MX, TXT, CNAME, NS, SOA, CAA and common SRV names.

SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Syntax, the 10-lookup limit, key strength and policy, with builders to fix what’s wrong.

Mail servers and SSL. MX reverse DNS, plus certificate expiry, covered names and allowed issuers.

When to use this

After you change a DNS record, resolvers keep serving their cached copy until its TTL runs out. This shows which ones already have the new answer, and how long the rest may hold the old one.
